# NEON RAIN: visual style guide (from John's reference boards, 2026-10-07)

Reference stills (local only, not committed; film stills are copyrighted): `art/reference/ref_*.png`. Use them for mood and lighting only. Never copy a frame, a logo, a sign word or a character.

## The look in one line
Thick smoky haze, hard light sources you can see, warm sodium amber against cold teal, silhouettes against light, and neon that bleeds into the fog.

## Rules
1. **Haze everywhere.** Every interior and exterior has volumetric smoke or fog. Light beams are visible in the air. Distant objects fade into the haze.
2. **Visible light sources.** Lamps, sconces, searchlights, car lights and signs appear in frame and flare (soft bloom, lens glow, occasional anamorphic streak). Light comes from practical sources, never from nowhere.
3. **Two temperatures.**
   - Warm: sodium amber (~2000 K), incandescent (~2700 K), orange bar lights on walls.
   - Cold: teal-cyan haze, cold window light, screens.
   - Most frames mix both: warm practicals inside a cold room, or a cold exterior seen through warm blinds.
4. **Silhouettes.** Characters are often backlit: a dark figure against a bright window, hologram or haze. Faces get light only where a source hits them (a beam across the eyes, a flame from below).
5. **Venetian blinds and slats.** Hard striped shadows on walls, desks and faces.
6. **Neon.** Tube signs in pink, cyan, red, amber and green with made-up words and characters; they glow into the fog and reflect in wet ground. Street scenes are dense with signs, awnings, stalls and crowds.
7. **Holograms and screens.** Giant translucent holograms (pink, blue, red) and CRT/video screens with scanlines and slight color fringing. Screens light faces.
8. **Interiors.** Dark wood, cluttered retro-tech (CRTs, cables, gauges, old phones), patterned tile or concrete walls, brutalist corridors with strip lights, warm wall bars in cold rooms.
9. **Vehicles.** Police hover cruisers: bright head and spot lights, red and blue roof lights, searchlight beams cutting the haze.
10. **Signature shots.** Extreme eye close-ups with the city reflected in the iris (title or chapter transitions); a figure in a long coat seen from behind facing a vast lit space.
11. **Grade.** Deep blacks, lifted haze, film grain. Warm amber and teal are the dominant pair; red is for lips, blood and a few neon tubes.

## Apply to
Environments (fog volume + beams in every scene; neon density in streets), the post grade (warm/teal split, halation), characters (backlight rims; silhouettes in doorways), title and chapter cards (eye close-up transition), the alley and the rain market (dense neon street), the penthouse (cold brutalist interior with warm practicals).

## Art deco vocabulary (John, 2026-10-07; board: art/reference/ref_art_deco_elements.png, inspiration only)
Black + brushed gold + teal (jade) as the decorative palette for UI frames, signage, the Blue Orchid, the penthouse and the Arcology. The repeating parts:
1. Stepped corners (UI panel corners, door frames, mirror frames).
2. Sunburst crests (above the club stage, logo crowns, elevator doors).
3. Half-fans over doorways and windows.
4. Chevron rules (dividers in menus, wall trim, bar fronts).
5. Pointed gold dividers / spikes (sconces, menu separators).
6. Lozenge with a teal gem (buttons, badges, the Glass Saint's plinth).
7. Fluted piers / pilasters (club walls, penthouse columns).
8. Portholes in rounded-corner frames (doors, lounge windows, the jukebox).
9. Speed-line whiskers (transitions, title underlines, motion accents).
10. Ziggurat finials (building tops in the skyline, lamp tops, the Arcology).
11. Winged medallions (original designs only; the club crest, the police badge).
12. Frozen-fountain sprays (fountains, light fixtures, glass etching).
Use them in: the UI (menus, HUD frames, case file, jukebox), the Blue Orchid (stage arch sunburst, fluted piers, chevron bar front), the penthouse, elevator doors, signage borders. Keep them geometric and crisp; gold reads as warm amber light in the noir grade; teal gems double as the cold accent.
